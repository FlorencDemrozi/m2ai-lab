The m²AI BMD Lab is a shared, fully synchronized multimodal sensing environment at the
University of Stavanger. We work with partners who want to test new technology, build
datasets, train people, or run research projects together. The table lists the most common
ways to get involved. If your idea does not fit any of them, get in touch anyway.

## Ways to work with us

| Type | Option | What it involves |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Use the lab | Validate a sensor, wearable or algorithm | Test against optical motion-capture ground truth and other reference instruments, recorded on one shared clock. |
| Use the lab | Collect a multimodal dataset | Record in a controlled room; our researchers help with protocol design, synchronization and ethics approval. |
| Use the lab | Run a pilot study | Try a product or method here before taking it into a clinical or field setting. |
| Contribute | Equipment | Loan, donate or co-fund instruments. Contributed equipment is listed on this site with its source and stays available to the projects it was meant for. |
| Contribute | Expertise | Co-supervise student theses, give guest lectures, or join a study as a domain expert (clinical, sports, industrial). |
| Contribute | Problems worth solving | Bring a real-world question; we can turn it into a BSc or MSc thesis, a summer project or a funded research proposal. |
| Joint project | [Industrial PhD](https://www.forskningsradet.no/en/call-for-proposals/2026/industrial-phd-scheme-doctoral-projects-industry-2026/) | A doctoral candidate employed by your company and enrolled at UiS, with the lab as academic partner. Details below. |
| Joint project | Public-sector PhD | The same model for municipalities, hospitals and other public bodies. |
| Joint project | Research Council projects | Innovation and collaborative projects where a company or public body leads and the lab is the research partner. |
| Joint project | EU Horizon Europe | International consortia, with the lab as research partner. |
| Joint project | Contract research and student projects | A defined question, answered by our researchers or students. |

See the [equipment list](../devices/) for what is available today and what is on its way.

## Industrial PhD in short

The Research Council of Norway's Industrial PhD scheme funds a doctoral candidate employed by
your company. UiS provides the doctoral programme and supervision, the candidate spends at least
a year with us, and the research is on problems that matter to your company.

| Topic | 2026 call (check the Research Council's page for the current rules) |
| --- | --- |
| Who applies | The company, which owns the project. It must be registered in Norway and have at least two full-time employees when it applies. |
| The candidate | Has a master's degree, is named in the application, and is employed by the company for three or four years. |
| Funding | Up to 50 % of the approved project costs. Applications are processed in order of receipt until the call's budget is used up. |
| Our part | Help shape the project idea, find a candidate among our master's students, and write the application together. |

## How to start

1. **Tell us what you need.** A short description of your technology, question or idea is enough.
2. **Meet us.** We discuss your idea and show you the lab.
3. **Agree on the form.** Lab use, thesis, PhD or funded project — with a written agreement on
   data, publication and intellectual property where needed.
