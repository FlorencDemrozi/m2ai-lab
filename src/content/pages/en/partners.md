The m²AI BMD Lab is a shared, fully synchronized multimodal sensing environment at the
University of Stavanger. We work with partners who want to test new technology, build
datasets, train people, or run research projects together. Below are the most common
ways to get involved. If your idea does not fit any of them, get in touch anyway.

## Use the lab

- **Validate a sensor, wearable or algorithm** against optical motion-capture ground truth
  and other reference instruments, recorded on one shared clock.
- **Collect a multimodal dataset** in a controlled room, with our researchers helping on
  protocol design, synchronization and ethics approval.
- **Run a pilot study** before taking a product or method into a clinical or field setting.

See the [equipment list](../devices/) for what is available today and what is on its way.

## Contribute to the lab

- **Equipment.** Loan, donate or co-fund instruments. Contributed equipment is listed on this
  site with its source, and stays available to the projects it was meant for.
- **Expertise.** Co-supervise student theses, give guest lectures, or join a study as a
  domain expert (clinical, sports, industrial).
- **Problems worth solving.** Bring a real-world question; we can turn it into a BSc or MSc
  thesis, a summer project or a funded research proposal.

## Industrial PhD

The Research Council of Norway's
[Industrial PhD scheme](https://www.forskningsradet.no/en/call-for-proposals/2026/industrial-phd-scheme-doctoral-projects-industry-2026/)
funds a doctoral candidate employed by your company and enrolled at a university. The lab
can be the academic partner: UiS provides the doctoral programme and supervision, the
candidate spends at least a year with us, and the research is carried out on problems
that matter to your company.

In short (2026 call; check the Research Council's page for the current rules):

- **The company applies** and owns the project. It must be registered in Norway and have at
  least two full-time employees when it applies.
- **The candidate** has a master's degree, is named in the application, and is employed by
  the company for three or four years.
- **Funding** covers up to 50 % of the approved project costs. Applications are processed
  in order of receipt until the call's budget is used up.

We are happy to help shape the project idea, find a candidate among our master's
students, and write the application together.

## Other joint projects

- **Public-sector PhD** — the equivalent scheme for municipalities, hospitals and other public bodies.
- **Innovation and collaborative projects** with the Research Council of Norway, where a company
  or public body leads and the lab is the research partner.
- **EU Horizon Europe** and other international consortia.
- **Contract research** and student projects for a defined question.

## How to start

1. **Tell us what you need.** A short description of your technology, question or idea is enough.
2. **Meet the team.** We match you with the researchers whose expertise fits, and show you the lab.
3. **Agree on the form.** Lab use, thesis, PhD or funded project — with a written agreement on
   data, publication and intellectual property where needed.

Find the right person on the [researchers page](../researchers/), or contact the lab's
principal investigator there.
